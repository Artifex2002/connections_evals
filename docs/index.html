01 / THE QUESTION

A final answer leaves
most of the story untold.

Two models can finish with the same score. One never finds a better solution. The other finds it, revises it, and loses it.

Connections gives us a controlled way to study the difference: sixteen words, four hidden groups, and a known solution against which to score each expressed hypothesis.

DISCOVERY ≠ PRESERVATION

A good state can disappear.

Peak quality and final quality measure different capabilities. Their gap identifies structure found and subsequently lost.

REVISION ≠ ERROR

More change isn’t always worse.

Churn can reflect productive exploration or unstable reasoning. Error persistence and structural coherence help tell them apart.

ACTION CHANGE ≠ ADAPTATION

A → B → A is still a loop.

A model can change every adjacent action while returning to a previously rejected guess. History matters.

THE BROADER QUESTION

What good is a better idea
if it never becomes a better action?

Reliable interactive reasoning requires more than discovery. It requires preserving useful structure, carrying it into action, and remembering what feedback has already ruled out.

Scope & limitations +

The study measures expressed hypotheses rather than latent computation. Stance classification has not yet been systematically validated against human annotations. Connections is a controlled task; transfer to broader agent settings remains to be established.

Multi-turn model coverage differs. Direct comparisons should use matched puzzles; feedback-conditioned results are descriptive, not causal. High revision alone does not imply poor reasoning.

What’s real on this page? +

The study counts and three-model comparison are taken from the supplied paper. The puzzle, hero illustration, and selectable replays are constructed demonstrations, not recorded model outputs. Replay quality is computed from their actual displayed partitions.

Author attribution, a publication-ready paper link, and verified experiment exports can be added when available. No publication or acceptance status is claimed.

Replacing the demonstration data +

The replay loads a separate data file, ready for curated real runs. Each export should include puzzle words and gold groups, ordered reconstructed partitions, official actions and feedback, model/run identifiers, and policy/version provenance.

Use the integration guide in the project source to prepare exports. Full databases are not required for this browser experience.
